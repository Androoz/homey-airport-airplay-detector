Bring an AirPort Express (2nd generation) into your Homey automations. The app detects when an AirPlay session connects or disconnects and exposes that state to Flow, making it easy to switch an amplifier to the correct input automatically.

Detection runs entirely on your local network without cloud accounts or external services. Homey Pro and the AirPort Express must be on a network where mDNS/Bonjour traffic can pass between them.
